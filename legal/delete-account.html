Delete your RosterBots account

RosterBots: Hangover Protection, by TurboLabs (Android package com.rosterbots.app)

Deleting your account permanently removes everything RosterBots has stored about you: every league you were watching, your ESPN sign-in, your protection settings, your activity and receipts, and this phone's push notification token. Your ESPN account itself is never touched — this only removes what RosterBots kept. This cannot be undone.

If you have the app

Open RosterBots → Settings → Delete my account, under Account. It deletes everything immediately and signs you out. This is the fastest way and needs nothing from us.

If you don't have the app, or can't open it

Email support@rosterbots.com from the address you used to claim your account (if you claimed one), with the subject "Delete my RosterBots account". Include whichever of these you can:

  • The email address you claimed the account with, if any
  • The ESPN league name and your team name, as shown on the app's Home screen
  • Roughly when you signed up

We'll confirm by email and complete the deletion within [PLACEHOLDER: e.g. 30] days.

What gets deleted

Your account record, your ESPN sign-in (encrypted, never stored in plain text), every league connection and its settings, the lineup activity and receipts RosterBots kept for you, and any push notification token tied to your devices. If you never gave us an email address, there is nothing further to remove beyond what's listed above.
